# IT Blackout

**IT Blackout** was the closing learning event of the MicroClub IT Section season. Its premise is that learning is only complete once you can teach it.

Members took one of two paths:

- **Hosts** picked a technical topic they did not yet master, studied it in depth, proved they were ready to teach it, then delivered a live workshop for the community.
- **Participants** attended the workshops, took part in follow-up discussions, solved the practical challenge that came with each workshop, and built up their impact through contributions.

Every workshop was followed by a discussion space for questions, resources and summaries, and by a small hands-on challenge to apply what was taught.

## What's in this folder

Each subfolder holds the material for one workshop topic: Computer Vision, gRPC, Neural Network, State Management and WebSockets.
